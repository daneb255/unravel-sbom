# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or experience level.

## Expected Behaviour

- Use welcoming and inclusive language
- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Accept constructive feedback graciously
- Focus on what is best for the community
- Show empathy towards other community members

## Unacceptable Behaviour

- Trolling, insulting comments, or personal attacks
- Deliberate intimidation or sustained disruption of discussion
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Any conduct that would be considered unprofessional in a work setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other official communication channel.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported to `d@bitzer.dev`. All reports will be reviewed promptly and treated with confidentiality.

Maintainers may remove comments or contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation for repeated or serious violations.
